# Evidence-led, direct scientific writing

Establish the contribution before polishing. State the strongest supported conclusion, why it matters, and what evidence supports its scope. Then organize the manuscript around that argument.

For baseline-centered module search, exploration may precede the narrative. After the retained combination is tested, connect the baseline's observed weakness, the modules' distinct roles and the resulting evidence into one argument. Module count is a configurable search target, not a novelty metric. Attribute transferred methods and use ablations/diagnostics to support their roles; do not retroactively describe an untested explanation as a prior prediction. See [model-grafting.md](model-grafting.md).

## Drafting order

1. Build a claim–evidence map: each central claim points to a figure, table, analysis, experiment or verified source.
2. Assign each result a purpose: establish the finding, distinguish a mechanism, demonstrate practical value, or constrain the conclusion. Keep the complete result record.
3. Draft a one-sentence contribution with its meaningful conditions. Choose verbs such as observes, supports, predicts or demonstrates according to the evidence.
4. Write connected paragraphs that develop the argument, then refine terminology, transitions and sentence length.
5. Inspect citations, numbers, comparisons, scope and material negative results after editing.

## Remove defensive and formulaic prose

- Replace generic openings and repeated declarations of importance with the actual problem and observation.
- Remove apologies, repeated disclaimers and hedges that add no information. Keep uncertainty that changes how the result should be interpreted.
- Prefer concrete mechanisms and actions to vague nouns, inflated adjectives or a list of fashionable model names.
- Avoid stock contrast formulas, redundant contribution lists and experimental diary narration.
- Move implementation detail to the place where a reader needs it; retain enough detail for reproduction.

Writing around a strength does not authorize changing the evidence. Preserve valid uncertainty, necessary assumptions, comparison conditions and conclusion-changing unfavorable findings. Do not select only the best seed or hide a relevant failed comparison. If an initially proposed story fails, revise the claim and run the required confirmation; keep post hoc choices traceable.

## Illustrative revision

The following is synthetic prose, not a report of a study.

Before: "Although our approach has several limitations, we believe it may potentially provide a novel and effective way to improve robustness in complex scenarios."

After, when supported: "The method reduces sensitivity to the specified measurement shift by estimating and removing its shared component. Independent measurements test whether that correction preserves the target signal."

The revision gains precision from an identifiable mechanism and test. It does not add an unmeasured improvement or erase a condition needed to interpret a result.

## Manuscript package

Prepare the main argument, figures/tables, methods, relevant supplementary evidence and verified references. Keep response letters referee-separated where applicable. Check the chosen venue's current instructions when preparing a submission. Accurate, clear prose is the objective; this workflow makes no AI-detector evasion or acceptance guarantee.
